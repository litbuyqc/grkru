The Oopbuy spreadsheet guide

From a long list
to a considered choice.

A useful list answers three questions: what is it, will it fit, and what might it cost in total?

01

Choose a category

Separate footwear, layers, everyday clothing and accessories. When reviewing Oopbuy finds, keep comparable products together instead of mixing unrelated items in one ranking.

02

Capture the evidence

Save the original listing, selected variant, relevant measurements and the date checked. Note whether images are studio references, seller photos or photos of the actual item.

03

Review before paying

Recheck availability, size information, return conditions and the full cost estimate. Keep unknown details marked as unknown until you can verify them.

A practical comparison framework

Compare the right details.

Use these fields in your own notes or spreadsheet. This page is a guide and category directory, rather than a downloadable product database.

What to record for each product type
Product type Record these details Resolve before ordering
Footwear Model, color, size system, size chart and available foot or insole measurements. Confirm the measurement method and whether the selected size matches the exact listing.
Tops & layers Chest width, garment length, shoulders, sleeves and stated fabric composition. Compare measurements with clothing you own and check whether the fit is intentionally loose.
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, leg opening and any information about stretch. Check whether waist measurements are taken flat, doubled or expressed as circumference.
Headwear Head circumference range, crown depth, brim shape and adjustment mechanism. Check whether the style is fitted or adjustable.
Accessories & jewelry Dimensions, stated materials, fastening type and care instructions. Ask for missing specifications; appearance alone cannot establish composition.

Make every column useful

A shortlist you can
actually use.

  • Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet: group comparable shoe styles and record the exact size system for each entry.
  • Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet: prioritize garment measurements and fabric notes over a size letter.
  • Oopbuy accessories spreadsheet: add dimensions, materials and closure details that photos may not reveal.

Quick answers

Before you build your list.

Clear answers to common questions about spreadsheet-based product research.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-reference list used to organize items someone is considering buying through Oopbuy. Useful entries record the original listing, product options, measurements, price notes and the date checked. A spreadsheet is a comparison tool, not proof of product quality or availability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and comparison guide. Its browsing links open CAFANS pages rather than a downloadable spreadsheet or an Oopbuy checkout.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item you own. Check the measurement unit and method, and record chest width, length, shoulder width or waist measurements as appropriate. A letter size alone is not enough.

What should I check before choosing shoes?

Check the exact model, color, size system, available measurements and return conditions. Use the listing's own size information and compare it with a pair that fits you well.

How do I estimate the total cost?

Record the item price and currency, then add applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and destination taxes or duties. Keep estimates separate from confirmed quotes and update them before paying.

Do reference images confirm stock or authenticity?

No. Reference images illustrate styles only. They do not establish current CAFANS inventory, seller authorization, authenticity or availability. Verify the actual listing and seller evidence separately.
